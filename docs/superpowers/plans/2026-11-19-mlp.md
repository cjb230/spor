in `models/mlp-mps-resumed.pt`. All checkpoints reload on CPU with 468,199 parameters
and record PyTorch 2.15.0. These are integration smoke tests, not strong players:
the ten-epoch model achieved 27.88% training versus 3.82% validation move accuracy.
